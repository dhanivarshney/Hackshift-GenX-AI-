## 📽️ Exact 7-Slide Pitch Script & Speaker Notes

---

### Slide 1: 01 / 07 • Title & Vision
- **Display:**
  - Event: HackShift Hackathon • IIIT Delhi
  - Project: **LinkX**
  - Theme: *“Building a Living Network, Not a Dead Directory.”*
  - Team: Dhani Varshney, Dipika Mourya, Ansh Kumar, Harsh Pal

  > In every college, including Tier-1 institutions, alumni portals are nothing more than a dead phonebook with 10,000 names and zero daily activity.  
  > We present **LinkX**: an autonomous intent and proof-of-work exchange where connections happen not just by who you know, but by who has the active intent and technical alignment to help you grow."*

---

### Slide 2: 02 / 07 • The Challenge
- **Display:**
  - Finding the right mentor takes more than a dead directory.
  - **01 Relevance Gap:** Students don't know who is actively open to mentoring or referring.
  - **02 Time-Consuming Search:** Manual browsing through hundreds of stale LinkedIn profiles with zero signal.
  - **03 Outreach Hesitation & Cold-DM Spam:** 98% of begging messages (*"Sir please refer me"*) are ignored by busy alumni.
- **Speaker Notes (20 seconds):**
  > *"Why do traditional portals and cold DMs fail? Because of three massive friction points:  
  > First, the relevance gap — students have no clue whose skills and current hiring slots match their goals.  
  > Second, manual search takes hours.  
  > And third, students resort to cold-DM begging on LinkedIn, which alumni ignore 98% of the time because they simply don't have time to review 5-page resumes."*

---

### Slide 3: 03 / 07 • Smart Workflow: Our Solution (LinkX)
- **Display:**
  - 1. **Understand Intent:** Natural-language career goal input.
  - 2. **Semantic Matching:** Aligns goals with live alumni hiring slots & company domain.
  - 3. **AST Proof-of-Work:** Scans GitHub repo architecture, unit test ratios (92.4%), and P99 latency (14ms).
  - 4. **1-Minute Dossier & 1-Click Referral:** Packages 60-second scorecard for instant ATS referral.
  - *Live Example Box: Student Goal ➡️ Consistent Hashing Repo in Go ➡️ 94/100 Readiness ➡️ Rohan Verma @ Swiggy triggers 1-Click ATS Referral.*
- **Speaker Notes (25 seconds):**
  > *"LinkX solves this through an end-to-end 4-step pipeline:  
  > The student describes their career challenge in natural language. Instead of a cold text, the student stakes their actual GitHub repository.  
  > Our AST engine parses their architecture, computes a 94/100 Code Readiness Score, and packages a verified 1-Minute Executive Dossier.  
  > The alumnus can evaluate this in under 60 seconds and approve a 1-click verified referral straight into their corporate ATS!"*

---

### Slide 4: 04 / 07 • Capabilities (Key Features)
- **Display:**
  - **Anti-Cold-DM Pitch Lab:** Zero raw begging text allowed; verified code staking.
  - **Explainable Recommendations:** Grounded in stack compatibility and shared university lineage.
  - **1-Click ATS Referral Radar:** Pre-formatted dossiers push directly to Workday, Lever, Greenhouse.
  - **Micro-Bounties & Capstones:** Paid startup feature tickets (₹5k–₹25k) and Google/Swiggy faculty-mentored research capstones.
- **Speaker Notes (20 seconds):**
  > *"Our capabilities are focused on removing friction:  
  > The Anti-Cold-DM Pitch Lab guarantees zero spam.  
  > The Alumnus Referral Radar respects alumni time with 1-click decisions.  
  > And our Opportunity Exchange lets alumni founders post paid micro-bounties so students earn stipends and prove their engineering skills on production tickets."*

---

### Slide 5: 05 / 07 • Differentiating Advantage
- **Display:**
  - **Traditional Directory (LinkedIn / College DB):** Stale keyword filters, 98% message drop rate, unvetted self-reported resumes, zero institutional outcome data.
  - **LinkX Autonomous Network:** Natural-language intent matching, zero-spam guarantee, verified AST code scorecards, 1-click ATS referrals, and live NIRF/NAAC accreditation telemetry.
- **Speaker Notes (20 seconds):**
  > *"Here is how LinkX stands apart: Traditional directories are passive phonebooks where students beg for referrals.  
  > LinkX is an active economic exchange. We don't ask alumni to read resumes — we give them proof of code quality.  
  > And we give universities audit-ready telemetry on placements and student bounty payouts."*

---

### Slide 6: 06 / 07 • Technology Stack & Live Prototype
- **Display:**
  - **Frontend:** Modern ES6+ SPA & React 19 / Vite, Tailwind CSS Cyber Obsidian design tokens, Lucide icons.
  - **Backend:** Node.js, Express REST API, ACID-safe JSON datastore.
  - **AI Engine:** GitHub REST API v3, AST Code Complexity & Readiness Scanner, Google Gemini LLM summarizer.
  - **Live Prototype:** Running live at `http://localhost:3000` with 6 interactive role gateways.
- **Speaker Notes (20 seconds):**
  > *"Our technology stack is built for speed and reliability:  
  > Node.js and Express REST backend, lightweight zero-bundle-overhead frontend with hardware-accelerated Tailwind styling, and a custom AST code evaluation engine integrated with GitHub's REST API.  
  > The entire engine is running live on our system right now across 6 distinct role gateways."*
