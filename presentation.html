04 / Alumnus Experience

Alumnus Referral Radar: Zero-Lag Decisions

Respecting alumni time while unlocking company referral bonuses without reputational risk.

1-Click Verified Referral Action

The alumnus reviews the 1-minute dossier and clicks "1-Click Verified Referral". LinkX generates an authenticated candidate package formatted directly for internal company ATS pipelines (Workday, Greenhouse, Lever).

Constructive Code Feedback Loop

If the code isn't ready for a referral yet, the alumnus provides 2 structured lines of technical guidance (e.g. "Add mutex lock on cache rebalance"). The student refactors code and can re-submit without spamming.

Reputation Staking & Zero Risk

Engineers at top tech firms protect their internal reputation. Because LinkX guarantees verified AST scores and passing unit tests, alumni refer with 100% conviction.

Referral Bonus Incentives

Alumni unlock internal company referral bonuses (ranging from ₹50,000 to ₹2,00,000) while actively mentoring junior builders from their own alma mater.
